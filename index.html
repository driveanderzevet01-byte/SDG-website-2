Incident report

Report an incident

Use one form for every incident so coordinators can respond and the network can see patterns. If someone is in danger now, call an emergency number first.

You can report anonymously. A trained coordinator will contact you privately, and SAPA 129 can help with protection and counselling.

What happens after you report

  1. The RT coordinator for that area receives the report and answers within 15 minutes (urgent) or 24 hours (non-urgent).
  2. For a crime, the coordinator contacts the police and the RW’s Bhabinkamtibmas officer.
  3. The incident is logged with a reference number, without the reporter’s name if anonymous.
  4. Patrol routes are adjusted if the same place appears in several reports.
  5. Reports are reviewed together at the monthly RW meeting.
